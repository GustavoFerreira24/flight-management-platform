# <span style="color:#E67E22">Glossary</span>

| <span style="color:#1F4E79">**_TEA_ (EN)**</span> | <span style="color:#1F4E79">**_TEA_ (PT)**</span> | <span style="color:#1F4E79">**_Description_ (EN)**</span>                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|:--------------------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Administrator**                                 | **Administrador**                                 | User with administrative privileges manages users. Helps creating the Backoffice Configuration.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Air Control Area**                              | **Área de Controlo Aéreo**                        | Defined geographic region responsible for managing and monitoring air traffic and ensuring flight safety.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Air Control Area Code**                         | **Código da Área de Controlo Aéreo**              | Unique identifier used to distinguish an air control area in the system.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Air Transport Company**                         | **Companhia de Transporte Aéreo**                 | Organization that operates aircraft, manages flights and employs collaborators and pilots.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Air Transport Company Collaborator**            | **Colaborador de Companhia de Transporte Aéreo**  | Collaborator whose job is to create the air companies configuration, creating flight routes, aircraft and manages pilots.                                                                                                                            |
| **Aircraft**                                      | **Aeronave**                                      | An individual aircraft belonging to a company, uniquely identified by its registration number and associated with a specific aircraft model and configuration.                                                                                       |
| **Aircraft Model**                                | **Modelo de Aeronave**                            | Type of aircraft defined by technical characteristics such as weight, fuel capacity, speed and aerodynamic properties.                                                                                                                               |
| **Aircraft Position**                             | **Posição da Aeronave**                           | Represents the state of an aircraft at a specific moment during a simulation. It includes coordinates, altitude, velocity and timestamp                                                                                                              |
| **Aircraft Registration**                         | **Matrícula da Aeronave**                         | Unique registration identifier assigned to an aircraft, used to distinguish it from all other aircraft.                                                                                                                                              |
| **Aircraft Variant**                              | **Variante de Aeronave**                          | Specific configuration of an aircraft model, defined by a combination of model and engine.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Airport**                                       | **Aeroporto**                                     | Location where aircraft will take off and land, identified by IATA and ICAO codes and defined by geographic and physical characteristics.                                                                                                            |
| **AIsafe**                                        | **AIsafe**                                        | AlSafe is an integrated air traffic management system that uses a custom DSL and physical simulations to ensure flight safety. It focuses on backoffice control, real-time monitoring, and collision avoidance through a scalable architecture.      |
| **AIsafe user**                                   | **Utilizador de AIsafe**                          | An Alsafe user needs to have an active security clearance that automatically expires at a given date. They also need to have periodic (per regulations 5 years) skills assessment. The Admin or the Backoffice operator can update this information. |
| **Altitude Slot**                                 | **Intervalo de Altitude**                         | Represents an allowed altitude band within a segment. Defined by minimum altitude, maximum altitude, and width.                                                                                                                                      |
| **Backoffice**                                    | **Backoffice**                                    | The administrative part of the system used by admins and backoffice operators to manage base data.                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Backoffice Operator**                           | **Operador de Backoffice**                        | User with operational privileges. Creates: aircraft models; aircraft and engine makers; airports; air traffic areas; air transport companies; engine models. Helps creating the Backoffice Configuration.                                            |
| **Cabin Configuration**                           | **Configuração da Cabine**                        | Distribution of seats in an aircraft by travel class, such as economy, business and first class.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Collaborator**                                  | **Colaborador**                                   | User associated with an air transport company or control entity, responsible for managing operational data.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Coordinates**                                   | **Coordenadas**                                   | Geographical position defined by latitude and longitude, used to represent the location of airports and route segments.                                                                                                                              |
| **Engine Model**                                  | **Modelo de Motor**                               | Engine type used in aircraft, characterized by properties such as power, efficiency and fuel consumption.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Flight**                                        | **Voo**                                           | Connection between two airports, identified by a unique flight designator and defined by type, departure and arrival information.                                                                                                                    |
| **Flight Control Operator**                       | **Operador do Controlo dos Voos**                 | User responsible for helping managing the flights. Tests flight plans and simulates the air control. He is also responsible for consulting weather data.                                                                                             |
| **Flight Designator**                             | **Designador de Voo**                             | Unique identifier of a flight composed of airline code, numeric identifier and optional suffix.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Flight Execution**                              | **Execução de Voo**                               | Represents the execution of a flight during simulation. Each execution runs a flight plan and produces position updates over time.                                                                                                                   |
| **Flight Leg**                                    | **Trecho de Voo**                                 | A single, non-stop segment of a flight between two airports, representing one take-off and one landing.                                                                                                                                              |
| **Flight Plan**                                   | **Plano de Voo**                                  | Structured description of how a flight is executed, including route, segments, fuel information and operational data. Basically represents a complete flight operation and may include multiple take-offs and landings.                              |
| **Flight Route**                                  | **Rota de Voo**                                   | Predefined path between two airports used by a company to create flights, identified by a unique route name.                                                                                                                                         |
| **Flight Type**                                   | **Tipo de Voo**                                   | Classification of a flight (e.g., regular or charter) defining its operational characteristics.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Geographic Boundary**                           | **Limite Geográfico**                             | Geographical delimitation that defines the extent of an air control area.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **IATA**                                          | **IATA**                                          | Standard three-letter code used to identify an airport, or two-letter code used to identify an air transport company.                                                                                                                                |
| **ICAO**                                          | **ICAO**                                          | Standard four-letter code used to identify an airport, or two-to-three-letter code used to identify an air transport company.                                                                                                                        |
| **Identifier**                                    | **Identificador**                                 | Used to represent flight identifiers and named entities.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Manufacturer**                                  | **Fabricante**                                    | Entity responsible for producing aircraft models or engine models.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Node / Junction**                               | **Nó / Junção**                                   | Connection point between segments that allows an aircraft to transition from one segment to another.                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Pilot**                                         | **Piloto**                                        | User responsible for helping managing the flights. Tests and registers flight plans. He is also responsible for consulting weather data.                                                                                                             |
| **Report**                                        | **Relatório**                                     | Summary of simulation results, including flight statuses and detected safety violations.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Safety Violation**                              | **Violação de Segurança**                         | Situation where aircraft violate minimum safety constraints such as distance or altitude separation.                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Security Clearance**                            | **Credencial de Segurança**                       | Authorization level that allows a user to access the system, valid for a limited period.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Segment**                                       | **Segmento**                                      | Linear path connecting two points in a route, defined by coordinates, altitude constraints and weather conditions.                                                                                                                                   |
| **Simulation**                                    | **Simulação**                                     | Process that executes multiple flight plans to analyze flight behaviour, detect safety violations and generate results.                                                                                                                              |
| **Skills Assessment**                             | **Avaliação de Competências**                     | Periodic evaluation of a user’s qualifications and readiness to perform regulated functions in the system.                                                                                                                                           |
| **Thrust**                                        | **Impulso**                                       | Parameter supplied by the manufacturer, usually at sea level and at two speeds: stopped and at cruise or maximum speed.                                                                                                                              |
| **User**                                          | **Utilizador**                                    | Someone with access to the system after authenticating into the system, it is identified by a unique valid email from the list of valid emails, as a name and phone number.                                                                          |
| **Weather Data**                                  | **Dados Meteorológicos**                          | Information about atmospheric conditions, such as wind speed and direction, used to support flight planning and simulation.                                                                                                                          |
| **Weather Measurement**                           | **Medição Meteorológica**                         | A single recorded weather value, such as wind speed, wind direction, temperature or pressure, associated with a specific location and moment.                                                                                                        |
| **Weather Person**                                | **Meteorologista**                                | Imports and registers weather data to the weather service                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Weather Provider**                              | **Fornecedor de Dados Meteorológicos**            | External source that provides weather data to the system.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
