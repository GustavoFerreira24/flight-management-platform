# Glossary

| **_TEA_** (EN)                         | **_TEA_** (PT)                                   | **_Description_** (EN)                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|:---------------------------------------|:-------------------------------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Air Control Area**                   | **Área de Controlo Aéreo**                       | Defined geographic region responsible for managing and monitoring air traffic and ensuring flight safety.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Air Transport Company**              | **Companhia de Transporte Aéreo**                | Organization that operates aircraft, manages flights and employs collaborators and pilots.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Aircraft**                           | **Aeronave**                                     | A specific aircraft belonging to a company, identified by a unique registration and associated with an aircraft model.                                                                                                                               |
| **Aircraft Model**                     | **Modelo de Aeronave**                           | Type of aircraft defined by technical characteristics such as weight, fuel capacity, speed and aerodynamic properties.                                                                                                                               |
| **Airport**                            | **Aeroporto**                                    | Location where aircrafts will take off and land, identified by IATA and ICAO codes and defined by geographic and physical characteristics.                                                                                                           |
| **Collaborator**                       | **Colaborador**                                  | User associated with an air transport company or control entity, responsible for managing operational data.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Coordinates**                        | **Coordenadas**                                  | Geographical position defined by latitude and longitude, used to represent the location of airports and route segments.                                                                                                                              |
| **Engine Model**                       | **Modelo de Motor**                              | Engine type used in aircraft, characterized by properties such as power, efficiency and fuel consumption.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Flight**                             | **Voo**                                          | Connection between two airports, identified by a unique flight designator and defined by type, departure and arrival information.                                                                                                                    |
| **Flight Leg**                         | **Trecho de Voo**                                | A single, non-stop segment of a flight between two airports, representing one take-off and one landing.                                                                                                                                              |
| **Flight Plan**                        | **Plano de Voo**                                 | Structured description of how a flight is executed, including route, segments, fuel information and operational data. Basically represents a complete flight operation and may include multiple take-offs and landings.                              |
| **Flight Route**                       | **Rota de Voo**                                  | Predefined path between two airports used by a company to create flights, identified by a unique route name.                                                                                                                                         |
| **Pilot**                              | **Piloto**                                       | User responsible for helping managing the flights. Tests and registers flight plans. He is also responsible for consulting weather data.                                                                                                             |
| **Segment**                            | **Segmento**                                     | Linear path connecting two points in a route, defined by coordinates, altitude constraints and weather conditions.                                                                                                                                   |
| **User**                               | **Utilizador**                                   | Someone with access to the system after authenticating into the system, it is identified by a unique valid email from the list of valid emails, as a name and phone number.                                                                          |
| **Weather Data**                       | **Dados Meteorológicos**                         | Information about atmospheric conditions, such as wind speed and direction, used to support flight planning and simulation.                                                                                                                          |
| **Administrator**                      | **Administrador**                                | User with administrative privileges manages users. Helps creating the Backoffice Configuration.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Backoffice**                         | **Backoffice**                                   | The administrative part of the system used by admins and backoffice operators to manage base data.                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Backoffice Operator**                | **Operador de Backoffice**                       | User with operational privileges. Creates: aircraft models; aircraft and engine makers; airports; air traffic areas; air transport companies; engine models. Helps creating the Backoffice Configuration.                                            |
| **Air Transport Company Collaborator** | **Colaborador de Companhia de Transporte Aéreo** | Collaborator whose job is to crete the air companies configuration, creating flight routes, aircrafts and manages pilots.                                                                                                                            |                                                                                               
| **Weather Person**                     | **Meterologista**                                | Imports and registers weather data to the weather service                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Flight Control Operator**            | **Operador do Controlo dos Voos**                | User responsible for helping managing the flights. Tests flight plans and simulates the air control. He is also responsible for consulting weather data.                                                                                             |
| **AIsafe user**                        | **Utilizador de AIsafe**                         | An Alsafe user needs to have an active security clearance that automatically expires at a given date. They also need to have periodic (per regulations 5 years) skills assessment. The Admin or the Backoffice operator can update this information. |
| **AIsafe**                             | **AIsafe**                                       | AlSafe is an integrated air traffic management system that uses a custom DSL and physical simulations to ensure flight safety. It focuses on backoffice control, real-time monitoring, and collision avoidance through a scalable architecture.      |     |
